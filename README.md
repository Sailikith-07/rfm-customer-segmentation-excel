# 📊 Customer Segmentation: RFM Analysis 

## 📖 Project Background
The store's sales are growing and total orders look healthy, but the customer base is quietly shrinking. New customers are becoming rare, and a large portion of existing customers are either disengaged or slowly drifting away. Stakeholders noticed the numbers don't add up—revenue is up, but something underneath feels off. They want to know why.

Using RFM segmentation across 4 years of transaction data, this analysis breaks down customer behavior patterns to give stakeholders a clear picture of where they stand today and what needs to change before it is too late.

**Insights and recommendations are provided on the following key areas:**
* **Customer Acquisition & Pipeline:** Analyzing the weakening inflow of new buyers.
* **Revenue Health:** Identifying the concentration of revenue among at-risk segments.
* **Seasonal Demand:** Evaluating peak order periods and marketing timing.
* **Product Category Depth:** Investigating high-spend verticals like Tech Hardware.

## 🚀 Executive Summary (FY 2018 Audit)
While 2018 shows a superficial revenue growth of **20.3% ($722K total)**, a strategic audit reveals that the store’s growth engine is stalling. Customer acquisition has reached a critical low, plummeting from **589 new customers in 2015 to just 11 in 2018**. 

The store is currently surviving on its legacy base, but this is a high-risk strategy: **53.91% of total revenue is now dependent on "Others" and "At Risk" segments** ($665K and $599K respectively). Without immediate intervention to reignite the acquisition pipeline and stabilize retention, the business faces a significant revenue cliff as these aging segments churn.

## 🔍 Insights Deep Dive

### 📉 The Acquisition Engine has Stalled
* **Observation:** New customer onboarding has seen a catastrophic decline over four years, dropping by over 98% from its 2015 peak.
* **The "So What":** The "New Customer" segment now represents the smallest portion of the revenue pie ($71K), indicating that current marketing is failing to attract fresh intent.
* **Impact:** The business is currently "harvesting" its existing database rather than planting for the future; once the current segments disengage, there is no pipeline to replace them.

![Photo 2](https://github.com/Sailikith-07/rfm-customer-segmentation-excel/blob/main/images/year-wise%20customer%20acquisition.png)

### ⚠️ Revenue is Concentrated in "At Risk" Portfolios
* **Observation:** The "Others" and "At Risk" segments dominate the revenue landscape, combined representing over $1.2M in potential churn risk.
* **The "So What":** While "Loyal Customers" contribute a healthy $583K, they are being vastly outspent by segments that are statistically drifting away.
* **Impact:** The high average monetary value ($1046.45) is currently driven by a shrinking number of people; the store is highly vulnerable to the loss of even a few key customers in these volatile segments.

### 💻 Tech Hardware: High Value, Low Frequency
* **Observation:** While Technology represents only 19% of the customer base, it drives the highest individual spend per transaction.
* **The "So What":** The 60% concentration of customers in "Office Supplies" provides volume, but the "Tech" segment provides the margins.
* **Impact:** The current failure to convert high-value Tech buyers into repeat "Loyalists" is a massive missed opportunity for Lifetime Value (LTV) expansion.

### 🕒 Extreme Q4 Seasonal Dependency
* **Observation:** Monthly trends show that sales remain relatively flat for the first eight months before surging in September and peaking in November.
* **The "So What":** The store is achieving its annual targets based on a 4-month window of high activity, leaving the infrastructure underutilized for the rest of the year.
* **Impact:** This "hockey stick" growth curve creates massive operational strain in Q4 and financial fragility during the Q1-Q3 "off-season".

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

* **Reignite Growth:** Launch a **referral program** specifically targeting "Champions" to unlock new customer inflow and diversify acquisition channels.
* **Stabilize Retention:** Deploy personalized **“We Miss You” campaigns** with high-value incentives to re-engage the $599K "At-Risk" segment before they transition to "Lost".
* **Capture Early Demand:** Move major marketing spend to **August Pre-Season campaigns** to capture early intent and smooth the revenue curve ahead of the Q4 peak.
* **Drive Repeat Orders:** Implement a **60-day Post-Purchase journey** for Tech hardware buyers, offering complementary office accessories to transition them into frequent shoppers.
